# Leadership asks: FOF planner, participant discussion, onboarding (briefs)

Captured 2026-09-29 from the user after talking to church leadership. Order: planner and discussion first; onboarding comes later.

## 1. FOF year planner (next)
- Each cycle is 17 weeks: **3 rest, 3 mobilisation, 10 classes (Sundays), 1 buffer**. There are **3 cycles a year** (51 weeks, one spare).
- There's a module to plan cycles into the church year and see an overview.
- **Adjustments ripple.** If a date moves, the dates after it re-flow. A church activity can take a class Sunday: the class moves to the next Sunday, and the buffer week absorbs the first shift. Beyond that, the end date and the following phases move too. It should do this "smartly", and warn when that squeezes the next cohort or pushes past 3 a year.
- Builds on the existing Cohorts (startDate / endDate, Weeks).

## 2. Discussion (interaction) space (next)
- For **participants and supports** primarily. Admins can see everything.
- Posts, comments and likes; **Report**; **Pin**.
- **Hub leads** see and moderate **their hub's people** (their hub's supports and those supports' participants).
- A **support or hub lead can remove** a post. The user wants removals to read as automatic, "so they don't think it's monitored", and wants **auto triggers that supports can set** (e.g. word filters, auto-hide after reports).
  - Recommendation given: word removed posts neutrally and truthfully, without naming who removed them (e.g. "Removed for not following the community guidelines"). Make the automatic side real (word filters, auto-hide after N reports) rather than labelling human removals as automatic.
- The existing staff Community (supports and admins) stays as it is.

## 3. Participant onboarding beyond the walkthrough (later)
