Getting Started

🔐 Signing in

Open the app and enter your email address or Nigerian phone number (e.g. 08012345678) and password, then tap Sign in. After signing in you land on your own home page — admins see the operations dashboard, support members see their personal home.

💡Support team members can self-register using the Create an account link. Admin accounts are created by the programme admin.

📱 Creating a Support account

  1. Tap "Create an account"Found at the bottom of the login screen.
  2. Choose Email or Phone NumberPhone must be in local format: 08012345678 — not +234.
  3. Enter your full name and a passwordPassword must be at least 6 characters.
  4. Tap "Create Account"You're created as a Support member. Your admin assigns you to a group and activity tags.

🧭 The guided tour & notifications

On your first visit you'll be asked to enable notifications, then a short in-app walkthrough highlights the key buttons on each page. You can skip the tour at any time and re-trigger it later from the ? help button on most pages.

How It's Organised

The whole app is built around one simple structure. Understanding it makes everything else click into place.

🏗️ Cohort → Group → Participant

Cohort→ Groups→ Participants
  • Cohort — one run of the programme (e.g. "Cohort 9"), made up of numbered weeks. One cohort is "active" at a time.
  • Group — a small group inside a cohort (e.g. "Group 16"). Each group is led by exactly one Support member.
  • Participant — a person in a small group. Each participant belongs to one group.
ℹ️Follow-up contacts are different. Contacts are prospective people being invited/registered. They are tracked separately from participants and can be assigned to a Support member to call and follow up.

🏷️ Activity tags

Activities on the schedule carry tags (e.g. "Prayer", "Class", "WhatsApp post"). Support members are assigned tags, and their schedule shows only the activities matching their tags — so everyone sees just what's relevant to them.

Roles & Access

There are three sides to the app. Your role decides which one you see when you log in.

👑

Admin

Programme Coordinator
  • Set up cohorts, weeks & the full schedule
  • Add participants & build groups
  • Assign Support leads & activity tags
  • Publish the schedule to Support
  • Oversee attendance, group meetings & faith projects
  • Run Hubs, pre-cohort trainings & follow-ups
  • Review change approvals, post announcements
  • Manage users, resources & settings
🤝

Support

Small-Group Leader
  • See their week-at-a-glance home page
  • View their schedule (once published)
  • Mark their own activities done
  • Run their small group in Meeting Mode
  • Take Sunday attendance for their group
  • Onboard their participants step-by-step
  • Register and follow up mobilisation contacts
  • See their Hub, if they're in one
🙋

Participant

Cohort Member
  • Sign in once a support gives them a login
  • Follow their journey week by week
  • Write private reflections
  • See their group, meeting time & support's contact
  • Mark Sunday attendance when it's open
  • Track their faith project
ℹ️An SOP Preparer role also exists for staff who help build the schedule and submit change requests for admin approval. It sits on the Admin side of the app with a narrower set of permissions.
For Coordinators

👑 Admin Side

Everything below is for admins. This is where the programme is set up and run: cohorts, the schedule, the people, and all the oversight tools. Support members do not see these screens.

A

Admin Dashboard & Navigation

📊 The Dashboard Admin

Your landing page. The operations snapshot shows pending approvals, resource count, number of Support members and announcements at a glance. Below it: today's activity snapshot with completion status, the approval queue, and recent announcements.

🗂️ The grouped sidebar Admin

Admin navigation is grouped into collapsible sections so it stays tidy:

  • Overview — Dashboard
  • Programme — Schedule (with Approvals, Rota and Activity overview as tabs on that page)
  • People & groups — Participants (with Attendance, Faith projects, Onboarding and Feedback as tabs), Groups (with Allocation and Group meetings as tabs), Supports, Hubs, Cohorts
  • Engagement — Follow-ups, Community
  • Administration — Users, Announcements, Resources (with Scriptures as a tab), Settings
💡Related pages share a tab strip at the top, so once you're on Participants, Groups or Schedule, the pages next to it are one tap away.

🛡️ Supports Admin

One page judging every Support against the agreed rules (set in Settings): weekly attendance and meeting reports submitted, and onboarding progress. Filter by Needs attention, Keep an eye on or On track to see who to check in with.

A1

Cohorts & Weeks

🎓 Managing cohorts Admin

On the Cohorts page you create and manage each run of the programme. One cohort is active at a time; everyone works against the active cohort. You can duplicate a cohort to reuse its structure, archive old ones, and permanently delete a cohort you no longer need.

📅 Weeks & week titles Admin

A cohort is made up of numbered weeks. You can add a week, give each week a descriptive title, duplicate a week's activities into a new week, and delete a specific week. Weeks always stay in numeric order.

💡Duplicating a week copies its activities — a fast way to set up a new week that's similar to a previous one, then tweak the differences.
A2

Participants & Groups

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Participants Admin

Add participants individually or import them into the active cohort. Search and filter by name, phone, or group, assign each person to a group, and manage their status (active / archived).

👥 Groups Admin

Create the small groups inside a cohort and assign one Support member to lead each group. Manage who's in each group, and filter to spot groups that don't yet have a Support lead assigned. You can also move a Support from one group to another at any time — open the group and choose Assign support, then pick a different person.

⚠️Pre-cohort trainings rule. The number of pre-cohort trainings a Support must attend before leading a group is set in Settings. If someone hasn't attended enough, the app blocks them from being assigned to a group and shows how many trainings they attended. You can still assign them — tap Override, give a reason, and save; the assignment then goes through.

🔀 Allocation Admin

The Allocation screen helps you place participants who aren't in a group yet. Drag them into groups manually, or use auto-distribute to spread unallocated participants evenly across groups in one click.

A3

Schedule & Publishing

🗓️ Building the schedule Admin

Add and edit activities for each day of each week. Every activity has a time, a description (with rich text — bold, italic, bullet and numbered lists), and one or more activity tags. You can filter the schedule by support tag or by a specific Support user to preview exactly what they'll see.

💡WhatsApp and Telegram are shown with their icons (and labels) right inside the activity text, so post instructions read clearly.

🚦 Publishing — the visibility gate Admin

The schedule starts in Draft. Support members cannot see any activities until you publish the schedule for the active cohort. Use the publish toggle at the top of the Schedule page — you'll be asked to confirm. You can unpublish at any time to take it back into draft.

⚠️If Support members report a blank "Schedule not published yet" message, that's expected until you publish. Publish when the week's plan is final.

📝 Approvals Admin

When an SOP Preparer proposes a change (add, edit, or delete an activity), it lands in the Approvals queue. Review each request and Approve to apply it to the live schedule, or Reject with an optional reason — the preparer is notified either way.

📈 Activity overview Admin · SOP Preparer

A completion breakdown per activity, per day — see at a glance who has marked an activity done and who hasn't, with filters for day, support tag, and individual Support user.

A4

Attendance & Group Meetings

✅ Sunday attendance Admin

Mark each participant Present, Late, or Absent for any week. Filter by group to review summaries and per-person status across the cohort.

⚠️The late rule. Present always counts as attended. Late or Left early only counts as attended once you mark it Excused with a note — otherwise it counts the same as a miss when the app judges a participant's standing.

🙏 Group meetings Admin

A grid of groups by week showing the prayer focus, whether the Support submitted their weekly meeting report, and how many participants joined — with a trend strip summarising completion across all groups. Open any cell to mark or fix a participant's Joined, Excused or Missed status yourself.

🌱 Faith projects Admin

Each participant has a faith project (a personal goal). View and edit any participant's project and track its status through the stages: Not drafted → Under refinement → Approved.

A5

Hubs & Trainings

A Hub groups several Support members under one Hub lead, so the lead can check in on their people and admins get a middle layer of oversight above individual groups.

🗂️ Hubs Admin

Create a Hub, add Support members to it, and pick one of them as the Hub lead. Each hub card shows its members, who leads which group, and this week's recap attendance — how many members the lead has marked present for the week, with a filter to spot hubs whose recap is behind.

  1. + New HubGive it a name.
  2. Manage membersAdd the Support members who belong to this hub. A support already in another hub shows "Moves from another hub" if you add them here.
  3. Pick leadChoose the member who leads the hub.
  4. Recap attendanceMark or check each member's weekly recap attendance yourself, if needed.

🎓 Trainings & get-togethers Admin

Create pre-cohort training sessions or get-togethers, picking the upcoming cohort they belong to. For each session, mark every Support member's attendance — Present, Late, Excused or Absent. How many trainings each Support attended feeds the pre-cohort trainings rule used when assigning them to lead a group (see Participants & Groups).

A6

Follow-ups & Onboarding

📞 Follow-ups Admin

Manage follow-up contacts (prospective participants), across four tabs: Overview (progress breakdown), Contacts (the full list), Message Bank (WhatsApp templates) and Issues (problems Support members have flagged, e.g. a wrong number). Track reply, call, and registration statuses; assign contacts to an owner to work; send templated messages; and multi-select contacts.

The list shows the active cohort's contacts by default — use the cohort filter to look at a different one.

🚀 Onboarding Admin

Track how far each group is through onboarding (percentage of participants fully onboarded), manage the message templates Support members use, set up onboarding coordinators, and watch the live activity feed of onboarding events.

A7

Users, Announcements, Resources & Settings

👤 Users Admin

Add users, assign roles (Admin, SOP Preparer, Support), and manage each Support member's activity tags. You can also reset a user's password directly from here — no email or OTP flow.

  1. Open UsersFrom the Administration section of the sidebar.
  2. Find the user and choose Reset passwordAn inline field appears in their row.
  3. Enter a new password (min 6 chars) and saveShare it with the user; they can log in immediately.

📢 Announcements Admin

Send a message to Support, Participants, or everyone. Everyone with notifications enabled receives it on their device, and it appears in their in-app announcements feed.

Choose "Or send to one person" to message just one Support member or one participant instead — this overrides any audience, tag, group or hub you picked above it. You can also narrow a Supports/Everyone announcement to one Hub, or a Participants announcement to one Group.

📚 Resources Admin

Manage the shared Resource Hub — add links or upload files (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, ZIP), with an option to notify everyone when you add something. All users can view; only admins add or remove. Scriptures is a tab on this page for uploading the participants' daily scripture images.

⚙️ Settings Admin

Manage reminder timings, the phone number the floating "Need Support" button messages, church departments, and AI help (opt-in end-of-programme summaries for participants, recap drafts and feedback themes). This is also where the programme rules live — the thresholds the app uses to judge participants and Supports, including the Sunday register's open window and the minimum pre-cohort trainings for a Support to lead a group.

For Small-Group Leaders

🤝 Support Side

Everything below is for Support members. Your screens are focused on your group and your own activities — clean and uncluttered. You won't see any of the admin setup tools.

S1

Home & My Schedule

🏠 Your Home page Support

A week-at-a-glance: where you are in the cohort (e.g. "Week 1 of 10"), how many activities you have today, your next group meeting, faith-project progress for your group, and the latest announcements. A shortcut opens the shared Sunday attendance register. Quick links jump you straight to your group call, resources, Mobilisation and profile.

📅 My Schedule Support

Your activities for the week, filtered to your activity tags so you only see what's yours. Switch between Today, Next Days, and Full Week.

⚠️If you see "Schedule not published yet", your coordinator is still finalising the plan. It will appear as soon as they publish it.

☑️ Mark activities done Support

Tap Mark done when you finish an activity — it saves instantly. Tap again to undo. Your admin sees this completion roll up in the Activity overview.

S2

My Group

📞 Your group call Support

Set or update your group's meeting day, time and call link — participants see this on their side too.

🌱 Faith projects Support

For each participant in your group you can view and update their faith project status (Not drafted → Under refinement → Approved), reply to encourage them, and keep their goal moving forward. If something's wrong with a participant, flag a concern and Operations is told straight away.

🙏 Meeting Mode Support

Walks you through your weekly group meeting step by step: Attendance (mark each participant Joined, Excused or Missed), Prayer (pick this week's prayer focus), Recap, Notes, then Submit. Submitting is what shows your group as done for the week on the admin side.

📋 Quick actions Support

Copy a participant's phone number in one tap to message or call them quickly.

S3

My Hub

If your admin has put you in a Hub, this page appears in your menu. Everyone in a hub sees the same overview; the hub lead sees extra tools too.

🗂️ Your hub Support

See your hub lead (with a one-tap WhatsApp link), your fellow Support members and the groups they lead, and your hub's meeting day, time and call link.

💬 Messages from the lead Support

Read messages your hub lead posts and tap Got it to acknowledge one. The lead can see who has and hasn't acknowledged each message.

✅ My attendance Support

See how your hub lead has marked you present for each week's recap, and for any pre-cohort training or get-together session.

👑 Lead-only tools Support

If you lead the hub, four extra tabs appear:

  • Recap attendance — mark each member present, late or absent for the week's recap.
  • Trainings & get-togethers — mark each member's attendance at a pre-cohort training or get-together session.
  • Notes — keep a private note on any member; only you and admins can see it.
  • Message hub — post a message to everyone in the hub. You can also edit or delete any message in the hub, including one a member posted.

You also set the hub's meeting day, time and call link from the overview tab.

S4

Attendance & Onboarding

✅ Take Sunday attendance Support

Tap Start attendance to open the register for the week — this also starts a countdown (set by your admin, e.g. 15 minutes) shown to you and to participants in their own app. Mark each of your group members Present, Late, Absent or Excused while it's open. You can add a private note on any participant — anything worth remembering.

⚠️Once the countdown ends, the register locks — you can still move an Absent mark to Late or Left early, but nothing else changes. It's then sent automatically at noon on Sunday, or once your admin sends the report.

🚀 Onboard your participants Support

Work through each participant's onboarding checklist: Contacted → Added to group → Introduction done → Venue acknowledged. Send a ready-made welcome message and copy their number right from the screen. Each saved step shows a spinner only on the box you tapped, so you can update several quickly.

ℹ️If you're an onboarding coordinator, you'll also see a section for sending coordinator-to-support onboarding prompts.
S5

My Follow-ups & Profile

📱 Mobilisation Support

Two jobs in one page. Registration is for signing up people you meet — fill in their details, or share the registration link so they can sign up themselves. Follow-ups lists the contacts assigned to you to contact. Right after each message or call, update their reply, call, and registration status — the app applies sensible cascades automatically (e.g. marking someone Registered also marks them Replied). Send templated WhatsApp messages, and use the ⋮ menu to report an issue with a contact or export your contacts.

Follow-ups shows your current cohort's contacts by default. Turn on Show past cohorts to see everyone else too.

👤 Profile & reminders Support

View your details and activity tags, and set how you want to receive reminders and notifications.

Community

Community is shared by Admin and Support — it's where the whole team talks. (It used to be called the Hub forum; same place, new name.)

💬 Topics Everyone

Start a topic to ask a question, share an idea or post an update for the team. Open topics are still going; Closed ones are settled but you can still read them. Tap a topic to read the replies, add your own, and see who liked it.

5

Participant App

Participants get their own, simpler side of the app to follow their journey through the programme.

🔐 Logging in Participant

A participant doesn't sign themselves up for the app — a Support or admin registers them first, then sends them their login details from their profile page. They sign in the same way as everyone else: email or phone number, plus a password.

🏠 Home Participant

Shows their progress through the programme with a shortcut to their group, a join-call button when their group has a call link, a new Inspirational Scripture every day from 2:00 PM, and what this week is about with a link to the recap and their reflection.

📖 My Journey & reflections Participant

Each week they can read the class recap and write a private reflection. Reflections are private — only the participant can read what they wrote; their Support only ever sees when they wrote it, never the content.

✅ Attendance Participant

A tab on My Journey shows their attendance record week by week. While the Sunday register is open, a countdown card appears on Home and here too — a live nudge to let their Support know they're present.

👥 My Group & Faith Project Participant

My Group shows their next meeting, the call link, their Support and group members, with a way to message their Support if they need help. Faith Project shows the one thing they're trusting God for, with space for their Support to reply and encourage them.

👤 Resources, Feedback & Profile Participant

Resources holds guides and class materials shared with participants. Feedback is completely anonymous — nobody can see who submitted it. Profile is where they update their details and photo, and choose when they're reminded about their group meeting.

Installing the App

📲 Why install?

FOF Ops is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Installing it to your home screen means it opens full-screen like a native app, loads instantly, and delivers notifications reliably. An Install banner appears in-app while you're using it in a browser — and disappears once installed.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open in Chrome and log inYou'll see the in-app Install banner. Chrome may also show its own install card at the top.
  2. Tap "Install"Or use Chrome's three-dot menu → Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. ConfirmThe FOF Ops icon appears on your home screen.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari only)

  1. Open in SafariChrome on iOS cannot install PWAs.
  2. Tap the Share buttonThe box-with-arrow icon.
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" → Add
💡Always open the app from the home-screen icon — not the browser — for the most reliable notifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

I can't log in — what do I do?
Use the correct email or phone number (format: 08012345678, not +234). Forgot your password? Contact your programme admin — they reset it instantly from Users. You'll be back in within a minute.
My schedule is empty / says "not published yet"
Support members only see activities after the admin publishes the schedule for the active cohort. Until then you'll see the "not published yet" message — that's normal. If it stays empty after publishing, your activity tags or group may not be set; contact your admin.
How does the Cohort → Group → Participant structure work?
A Cohort is one run of the programme made of numbered weeks. Inside a cohort are Groups; each group is led by one Support member and contains Participants. Follow-up contacts are separate — they're prospective people being invited, not participants.
What's the difference between a Participant and a Follow-up contact?
A Participant is someone already in a small group, taking part in the programme. A Follow-up contact is a prospective person being called/registered. Contacts can be assigned to a Support member to work; once registered, they may become participants.
I marked an activity done by mistake
Tap "Mark done" again to undo it. It saves instantly each way.
How do I publish or unpublish the schedule? Admin
On the Schedule page, use the publish toggle at the top and confirm. Publishing makes the schedule visible to Support; unpublishing returns it to draft and hides it again.
I'm not getting push notifications
Check you tapped Allow when prompted. If you dismissed it, go to phone/browser Settings → Site Settings → Notifications and allow FOF Ops. Make sure Do Not Disturb is off. After reinstalling the app, open it once to refresh your subscription. Notifications go to your most recently registered device only.
The Install banner keeps showing
That's by design — in a browser the banner reminds you on each visit. It disappears for good once you install the app to your home screen and open it from there.
The app shows old data
Pull down to refresh. If a "New version available" banner appears, tap Refresh to update. If data is still stale, check your internet connection.
I marked someone Late — does it still count as attended?
Present always counts. Late or Left early only counts as attended once an admin marks it Excused with a note — otherwise it counts as a miss when the app judges a participant. Ask your admin to excuse a late mark if there's a good reason.
What's a Hub, and why can't I see My Hub? Support
A Hub groups several Support members under one Hub lead for oversight and support. My Hub only appears in your menu if your admin has put you in one for the active cohort — not every Support is.
I can't be assigned to lead a group Admin
Your programme requires a minimum number of pre-cohort trainings before a Support can lead a group (set in Settings). If someone hasn't attended enough, assigning them is blocked — an admin can still go ahead using Override, with a reason.
