Your Role in the Hub

What's your role?

The basics

How it fits together

A cohort splits into hubs. Supports look after small groups, and each hub meets every Sunday.

The basics
Participant support
Has a group. Runs their weekly group call.
Hub Lead
Leads the supports in a hub. No group of their own.
Operational support (IT)
Tech help for 2 or more hubs. No group.

Three kinds of support

Most supports have a group. Hub Leads and IT don't.
