# Teen age bracket is "10 - 17"; an age-range change now moves the person

## Summary
- The teen bracket is renamed from "18 and below" to **"10 - 17"**; 18 is an adult and the first adult bracket is
  **"18 - 24"** (was "19 - 24"). FLOW_MAP rule 8 is rewritten.
- Changing a current-cohort participant's age range across the teen line now converts them: to teen (adult groups left,
  contact becomes TEENAGER, same-gender Teen Support assigned) or back to adult. FLOW_MAP rule 40.
- The Participants form locks the age range while a date of birth exists (the date would overwrite it) and says what a
  teen/adult switch does. The Participants table shows an "Age range" column in place of "Pre-cohort".

## Decisions and why
- The form still says "18 and below" and "19 - 24"; those answers map to "10 - 17" and "18 - 24". Changing the form is the
  form owner's job. Releasing before the form is reworded is safe: old answers are still read as teen. The only effect is
  that an 18-year-old who ticks "18 and below" is treated as a teen until an admin changes their age range. Waiting was
  caution, not a technical need.
- The safeguarding policy wording ("under 18") is a separate document and still needs its Department Head sign-off.
- Under-18 anyone already grouped as a teen is not moved back automatically at 18 unless the cohort ends or an admin edits.

## How it was tested
Rolled-back SQL tests for conversion, revert, notifications and the phone index; browser tests with a mocked backend
for the lock, hint, toast and table column. Not run against a real admin login.

## Open items
- Fourteen of fifteen teen participants have no date of birth, so any who are 18 cannot be found automatically; an admin
  changes their age range to "18 - 24" to revert them.
- Two participants labelled "10 - 17" belong to an old cohort and sit in old adult groups; left alone on purpose.
- About 160 participants have no age at all; variants such as "25-34" remain in older rows.
- Frontend `normaliseAgeRange` accepts every old spelling, so mixed data still renders correctly.

- A date of birth for a child aged 5 to 9 still gives "10 - 17" (the old rule accepted anyone from 5); left as is.
- The relabel migration was checked live afterwards: the one teen with a date of birth stayed "10 - 17", so nobody was split between teen contact and adult label.

## Gotchas
- `supabase-api.ts` contains non-text bytes; edit it as bytes, not with a text-only tool.
- The phone index exempts teens by label; any future relabel must update it first.
